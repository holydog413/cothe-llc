4. Authorization

Client hereby authorizes COTHE LLC and its agents, employees, and independent contractors to access and assess the in-scope systems solely for the purposes and duration stated in this Agreement. Client understands that assessment activity may generate security alerts, log noise, or temporary load on systems, and that Client is responsible for notifying its own security team / MSSP as appropriate so authorized testing is not treated as a hostile incident.

5. Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the total liability of COTHE LLC, its members, managers, agents, employees, and contractors arising out of or related to this engagement — whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise — shall not exceed the fees actually paid by Client to COTHE LLC for this specific engagement. In no event shall COTHE LLC or its agents be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, lost data, business interruption, or reputational harm, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

6. No Warranty of Completeness

Security assessments are point-in-time evaluations. COTHE LLC does not warrant or guarantee that every vulnerability, misconfiguration, or security issue will be discovered, that systems will be free of risk after testing, or that future attacks will be prevented. Findings reflect what was reasonably identifiable within the agreed scope, methods, and time window.

7. Client Authority & Indemnification

Client represents and warrants that the person signing this Agreement has full authority to bind Client and to authorize the assessment of all listed in-scope assets. Client agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless COTHE LLC and its agents, employees, and contractors from and against claims, damages, losses, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys’ fees) arising from: (a) testing of systems Client was not authorized to include in scope; (b) Client’s misrepresentation of ownership, authority, or scope; or (c) Client’s failure to notify stakeholders or obtain required third-party consents.

8. Work Product & Use

Deliverables (reports, summaries, recommendations, and related work product) are prepared for Client’s internal use in improving its security posture. Client may share them with its advisors, insurers, or auditors under confidentiality, but may not republish them as a public endorsement or as certification that systems are “secure.” COTHE LLC retains ownership of its preexisting tools, methods, and templates; Client receives a license to use the engagement-specific deliverables for its internal purposes.

9. Independent Contractor

COTHE LLC is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, employment, or agency relationship that would make Client responsible for the Service Provider’s employment taxes, benefits, or day-to-day supervision beyond the agreed scope and rules of engagement. Personnel performing work remain under the direction of COTHE LLC.

10. Governing Law & Venue

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction named above, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. The parties consent to exclusive venue in the courts described above for disputes arising from this Agreement, except that either party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to protect confidential information.

11. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other party’s non-public information confidential and use it only to perform or receive the services. Assessment findings about Client systems are Client confidential information. COTHE LLC may retain anonymized methodology notes that do not identify Client.
