FAQ

Straight answers about authorization, scope, and what to expect.

Will you test without the owner’s authorization?

No. COTHE LLC only performs network security assessments with written authorization from the owner or an authorized representative of the systems in scope. If you do not have authority to approve testing, we will not proceed. Use the authorization form before any assessment begins.

Is testing destructive?

Non-destructive checks are the default (discovery, careful scanning, configuration review, and similar work). Intrusive or disruptive testing is not authorized unless separately agreed in writing.

Who is this for?

Homeowners and small businesses that want a practical look at network exposure, Wi‑Fi hygiene, router/firewall basics, and endpoint hardening — with a clear fix list afterward.

What do I receive?

A plain-English report for your internal use: what was checked, what matters most, and recommended next steps. We do not claim every possible issue will be found; assessments are point-in-time.

How much does it cost?

Scope drives cost. Contact for a quote after describing your network and goals. This site does not list prices.

Where do I start?

Read the process above, then complete the signed authorization form. You can also reach out through the contact section for a quote before authorizing.
