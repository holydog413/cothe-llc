Thanks for authorizing your assessment. Here's what you need to do — it depends on which check you chose.

1. Remote (outside-only) check

Nothing to install. While you are at the location being checked (on its Wi-Fi or wired network), open the authorization form and tap Use my IP. That fills in your internet address so we can check what the outside world can see.

2. Full home / business check

For a look inside your network, we use Tailscale — a secure, free (personal plan) connection you control and can turn off any time.

  1. Create a free Tailscale account at tailscale.com (sign in with Google, Microsoft, or Apple).
  2. Install Tailscale on a computer that stays at the location:
    Windows Mac iPhone / iPad Android
    Other systems: tailscale.com/download
  3. Sign in to the app with the account you just made, and leave it connected.
  4. Share the device with COTHE. In the Tailscale admin page, open Machines → the “…” menu on your device → Share, and enter COTHE's Tailscale email: [COTHE Tailscale email — coming soon].
    Don't have it yet? No problem — we'll send you our Tailscale invite after payment.
  5. When the assessment is done, you can remove the share (or uninstall Tailscale) at any time.
