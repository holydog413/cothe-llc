Next: what to install

Thanks for authorizing your assessment. Here's what you need to do — it depends on which check you chose.

1. Outside-in check (internet view)

Nothing to install. While you are at the location being checked (on its Wi-Fi or wired network), open the authorization form and tap Use my IP. That fills in your internet address so we can check what the outside world can see.

2. Inside-out check (your internal network) — Tailscale on an always-on PC

If your quote includes the internal (inside-out) network assessment, we check your network from the inside — the same safe, look-don't-touch checks, aimed at the computers, cameras, printers, and other devices on your Wi-Fi/wired network. To reach them we use Tailscale, a free, well-known secure connection app. You stay in control: you can turn it off or uninstall it any time, and we only ever check the network range you wrote on your authorization (for example 192.168.1.0/24).

What you need: one Windows PC at the location that stays on (a desktop that isn't put to sleep is ideal) and about 10 minutes. We never ask for your passwords — not on this page, not by phone.

🛡️ Your safety
  • What gets installed: only the official Tailscale app from tailscale.com/download — or nothing at all if you choose a COTHE drop box.
  • Limited access: COTHE can reach only the LAN range on your signed authorization. Tailscale rules block us from everything else, and your PC can't reach COTHE or other clients.
  • Look-only: no logins, no password guessing, no exploits, no changes to your devices.
  • We leave: COTHE removes your device and access when your report is delivered and keeps none afterward. You can log out of Tailscale at any time to cut the connection instantly.
  • We never ask for your passwords.

Step 1 — Install Tailscale

  1. On the always-on PC, go to tailscale.com/download (choose Windows) and run the installer (click Yes when Windows asks for permission).
  2. A small Tailscale icon appears near the clock (bottom-right; you may need to click the ^ arrow to see it).
Download Tailscale (official site)

Step 2 — Join COTHE's network

Your COTHE contact sends you a one-time invite link (or a short setup key) after your quote is signed and paid.

  • Invite link: open it on the PC and click Accept, then sign in when Tailscale asks. Invite: ask your COTHE contact for the invite
  • Or setup key: open Command Prompt as administrator (Start → type cmd → right-click → Run as administrator) and paste the line from Step 3 — it includes the key.

Step 3 — Share your network range (subnet route)

This tells Tailscale that COTHE may reach your local network through this PC. In Command Prompt (administrator), paste the line your COTHE contact sends — it looks like this, with your range from the authorization:

tailscale up --advertise-routes=192.168.1.0/24 --authkey=YOUR_TAILSCALE_AUTH_KEY

Already joined with an invite link? Leave off the --authkey=… part. Not sure what your range is? In Command Prompt type ipconfig: if your IPv4 address is 192.168.1.25, your range is usually 192.168.1.0/24. Your rep can confirm.

Then keep the PC on and Tailscale showing Connected, and set the PC to never sleep during the assessment (Settings → System → Power → Sleep: Never).

Step 4 — COTHE approves the route (our step)

We approve your network range in COTHE's Tailscale admin console and confirm it matches your signed authorization. We'll let you know when the check runs. If anything doesn't match, we stop and contact you — we never scan outside your authorized range.

Step 5 — Removing Tailscale when we're done

  1. Right-click the Tailscale icon → Log out (this disconnects COTHE immediately), or
  2. Uninstall it: Start → Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Tailscale → Uninstall.

We also remove your device and route from COTHE's network on our side once your report is delivered.

Prefer a mailed drop box instead?

No always-on PC, or rather not install anything? COTHE can ship a small, pre-configured mini PC / Raspberry Pi. You plug it into power and your router; it connects to COTHE's Tailscale network by itself, we run the inside-out check, and you mail it back in the prepaid box. Ask your salesperson — it's noted on your quote and authorization (“prefer drop box”).
